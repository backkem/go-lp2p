OSP presentation over the LP2P API

Two “devices” in frames, connected by lp2p-shim over postMessage. The right frame is a presentation controller, the left a receiver. They speak the Open Screen application protocol (osp-app) over an LP2PDataChannel or an LP2PQuicTransport. Pairing dialogs are skipped (auto=1).

Transport: LP2PDataChannel · LP2PQuicTransport
