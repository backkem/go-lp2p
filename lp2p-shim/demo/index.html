lp2p-shim demos

An experimental polyfill for the Local Peer-to-Peer API. It is not secure: pairing is simulated and nothing is encrypted beyond what the transport does.

  • Two frames, one page: postMessage transport, full permission/picker/PIN flow.
  • WebRTC chat: open this link in two tabs of the same browser (signaling over BroadcastChannel, data over RTCDataChannel).
  • WebRTC between two devices: open on two devices on the same LAN and copy the codes across by hand. Needs HTTPS (or localhost) on both.
  • postMessage chat: open in several tabs.
  • WebTransport in two frames: LP2PQuicTransport and LP2PQuicTransportListener: stream echo, datagram pings, a 1 MiB unidirectional upload.
  • WebTransport over WebRTC: open in two tabs, make one discoverable and connect from the other. Datagrams go over an unreliable, unordered RTCDataChannel.
